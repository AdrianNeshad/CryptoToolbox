Proton Wallet Mnemonic Decryptor

Decrypt the BIP39 recovery phrase stored by the Proton Wallet app from the values in your device keychain. Everything is decrypted locally in this window — nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

1 User PGP private key userPrivateKey

The armored OpenPGP private key from the keychain account userPrivateKey. You can paste more than one block if your account has several keys. Pasting it straight from a keychain or JSON export — with escaped \n and surrounding quotes — works too.

2 Key passphrase userPassphrase

The value from the keychain account userPassphrase. This is the already-derived key secret that unlocks the private key — paste it exactly, not your Proton login password.

3 Wallet key proton_wallet_k_provider_key

The value from the keychain account proton_wallet_k_provider_key. Paste the whole JSON array as-is, or a single -----BEGIN PGP MESSAGE----- block.

4 Encrypted mnemonic proton_wallet_mn_provider_key

The value from the keychain account proton_wallet_mn_provider_key. Paste the whole JSON array as-is, or a single encrypted mnemonic value.


            

                The other keychain accounts you may see — proton_wallet_address_provider_key
                (email address keys) and proton_wallet_app_k_is_user_eligible (a feature flag)
                — are not needed to recover the mnemonic.
